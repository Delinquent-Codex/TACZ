Adapted on 2026-09-09: IFPAnimationInstance and IFPGeoItemRenderer relocated
to TACZ's API; FirstPersonRenderHandler's switching state extracted into
FirstPersonItemState and target event/render adapters. TACZ retains its own
server swap notification and camera hooks. Target submission collectors
replace removed rendering buffers. This is the TACZ-used first-person
surface, not a general replacement distribution of SimpleBedrockModel.
